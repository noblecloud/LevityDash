# stars.csv

The Yale Bright Star Catalogue, 5th revised edition (Hoffleit & Warren, 1991), the stars of visual magnitude 5.8 and
brighter: J2000 right ascension and declination in degrees, V magnitude and a colour temperature in kelvin.

Taken from Bretton Wade's JSON conversion, https://github.com/brettonw/YaleBrightStarCatalog (MIT licence,
Copyright (c) 2016 Bretton Wade), itself from the catalogue held by the NASA Astronomical Data Center and the CDS,
which is free to use. Coordinates were converted to degrees; stars fainter than 5.8 were left out.
